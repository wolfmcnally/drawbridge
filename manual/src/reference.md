Inspect reports processability; verify checks a retained mirror against its original. Configuration restrictions such as local-only and no-AI remain authoritative.

PDF citation metadata binds physical page ordinals to the original digest and records code-point spans. Use header-aware section APIs for authoritative slices. Combined Markdown is untrusted presentation, not a sanitizer or secure viewer. Keep original documents and private notes/evidence in their own custody.

The retained repository product guides provide installation, examples, configuration, troubleshooting and licensing detail. This local manual does not enable hosted documentation publication.
