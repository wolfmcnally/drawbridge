During a methodology retrofit, inspect the complete production close-output inventory before the first candidate is qualified. The receiving declaration omitted the donor’s exact canonical execution-ledger bookkeeping path. Finalization correctly retained that ledger, but its previously absent file became active and invalidated the accepted product identity immediately before close. Compare generated destinations with declared classifications and their production validators, preserving unknown paths as active and the declaration itself as active. Correct only a reviewed exact classification; never conceal a canonical ledger with an ignore rule or broaden exclusions to make acceptance pass.
