Before making a Drawbridge release finding a blocker, identify the exact required acceptance property and the artifact or execution surface it applies to. The maintainer checkout must retain full private historical proof and pass its complete gate; public product tests and fresh wheel/source installation prove different properties. A fresh public clone cannot replay the private methodology audit, and that limitation must be documented, but it is not an additional release criterion. The earlier optional assessment was incorrectly elevated into a release hold and an upstream redesign proposal. Preserve its observed refusal and the corrective ruling, keep the lesson local, and do not publish private proof or propose Starter changes from this task.
