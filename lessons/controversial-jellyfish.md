When a recipient relocates pinned references, audit both the catalog reader and the document authoring/registration writer. The first adaptation changed readers while leaving writer destinations at the old namespace. A traversal-refusal test could still pass because every valid receiving destination was unsupported. Pair a valid create/register operation with the escape refusal, and preserve the independent reader diagnostics. This candidate concerns the transfer methodology and its portable catalog fixtures; it is not a request to reorganize product documents.
