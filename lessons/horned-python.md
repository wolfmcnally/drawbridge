After a staged qualification stops, inspect every remaining cheap assertion against artifacts that already exist before requesting another expensive window. A stopped installed-import probe had already left usable wheel/source artifacts, but their complete member oracle was not exercised until the next scheduled attempt and then exposed an unmodelled required public metadata member. Running the full read-only member audit immediately would have caught that independent tail defect without another scheduled failure. Preserve the first failure and distinguish artifact existence from successful complete qualification; do not weaken the oracle to conceal a mismatch. This candidate concerns orchestration preparation, not a proposal to broaden package contents.
